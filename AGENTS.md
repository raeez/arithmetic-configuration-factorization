# Agent Rules

## Authority and safety

`AGENTS.md` is the canonical local contract. `CLAUDE.md` points to this contract.
Inherit the applicable safety and research rules from `~/ecosystem/INVARIANTS.md`.
Read its Git safety, worktree ownership, evidence, attribution, and mathematical-repair rules before making changes.
Use `~/ecosystem/AGENTS-HARNESS.md` for host-specific execution guidance when needed.
Load business and publication guidance only when the task concerns those domains.

Work within assigned files and preserve concurrent changes. Use an isolated worktree for non-trivial edits.
Research or local build authority does not authorize cross-repository integration, signing, sending, or publication.
Report proved results, unresolved obligations, and the next discriminating step with their evidence.
A bounded investigation can finish with an unresolved theorem. Never present that theorem as proved.

## Golden rule: no project management at any scale whatsoever in the manuscript

Reader-facing mathematical sources contain mathematics only. This prohibition
applies to titles, abstracts, sections, theorem environments, proofs, remarks,
examples, captions, footnotes, tables, bibliography annotations, and PDF
metadata. Never insert agents, prompts, critiques, audits, repair history,
workflows, tasks, worktrees, commits, tests, dashboards, progress, ownership,
or drafting history. State theorem, conditional consequence, heuristic,
conjecture, and open problem at their exact mathematical strength.

The archive that suggested this subject is an untrusted lead, not evidence.
Verify every definition, theorem, normalization, citation, and dependency from
live sources. The objective is correct, rigorous, complete mathematics.

Use the structural virtues of Witten's major 1980s papers: begin with the
governing problem, introduce objects and maps before claims, expose the
decisive calculation, and distinguish proved results from open comparisons.
Do not imitate wording. Pass these rules to every subagent.

## Repository-specific proof boundary

The manuscript is `paper.tex`. Mathematical claims need independent proof or exact primary sources. Compilation is not proof.
